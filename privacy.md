# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

## About this tool

YouTube Autopilot is a personal-use project being configured
to prepare, upload, and schedule videos on its owner's
YouTube channel. It is not available for public registration.

## Google and YouTube data

With the owner's authorization, the tool may access channel
information and video metadata, and upload videos and their
associated titles, descriptions, and publishing settings.

The current requested permissions are youtube.upload and
youtube.readonly. The tool does not request the owner's
Google password.

## How data is used

Authorized data is used only to operate the owner's channel,
confirm uploads, and maintain operational records.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising profiles,
or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or
machine-learning models.

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to
the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its
Limited Use requirements.

## Service providers and security

The workflow may use hosting, automation, and AI service
providers to perform its stated functions. Data shared with
providers must be limited to what those functions require.

OAuth credentials and tokens must be protected and must
not be published in this website or its public repository.

## Retention and deletion

Operational data is retained only as needed for channel
operations and troubleshooting. The owner can delete
stored workflow files, logs, and credentials from the
services where they are held.

Deleting local workflow records does not delete videos
already uploaded to YouTube.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke authorization through their
Google Account's third-party connections settings:

https://myaccount.google.com/connections

Revocation prevents further authorized API access but
does not automatically delete previously stored records.

## Changes and contact

This policy must be updated before material changes to
data access or use. As the sole user, the project owner
manages privacy requests directly.
