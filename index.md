# YouTube Autopilot

A personal-use tool being configured to prepare, upload,
and schedule original videos on its owner's YouTube channel.

## Purpose

This tool is intended for its owner's use only. It is not
a public service and does not offer access to other users.

## YouTube access

The tool requests permission to upload videos and view
information about the owner's YouTube account.

Access is granted through Google's authorization process.
The owner can revoke that access through their Google
Account settings.
